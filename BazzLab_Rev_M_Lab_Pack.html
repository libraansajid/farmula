14. Cup-test method

StepMethod
1Condition A at 21–23 °C. Closed-cell B is chilled to 15–18 °C after HFO. Open-cell A and B stay 21–23 °C. Record actual temperatures.
2Dry cups. Weigh to 0.01 g. Prepare B exactly to 50.00 g in the order on the card. Water before catalysts.
3Homogenize B. For BX-01: HFO last, cap at once, hood on. If HFO flashes off, recap and reweigh — do not add water to compensate.
4Weigh A separately: 41.40 g (BX-01), 65.65 g (BX-02), or 196.6 g (OC-01). Do not default to 50 g A.
5Pour A into B. Mix 4–5 s at 2 500–3 000 rpm. Same mixer and time on every comparison cup.
6Clock cream, gel/string, rise and tack-free from mix start.
7Do not disturb after cream. Cut and inspect at 24 h.
8Record free-rise density, cell structure, collapse, shrinkage, friability and scorch.
