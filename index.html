HFO handling

HFO-1233zd(E) boils at about 19 °C. Chill the B cup to 15–18 °C. Weigh HFO last. Cap at once. Hood on. Mix 4–5 s so you are out before cream. If cream is still > 6 s: +0.15 CAT-T. If gel is still > 15 s: +0.15 PC-15. If it scorches or snaps too fast: −0.10 of each.

Do not pour 1:1

Bazz FoamMethod and first trials

Mix Method and Trial Matrix

Part B is the formula above. Part A is polymeric MDI. Bring both to temperature, then mix before cream.

Cup method

1. Condition A at 21–23 °C. Closed-cell B is 15–18 °C after HFO. Open-cell B stays 21–23 °C.

2. Weigh B, then weigh A, into dry cups. Do not default A to 50 g.

3. Pour A into B. Mix 4–5 s at 2 500–3 000 rpm for both OC and CC — cream is now 2–6 s.

4. Clock cream, gel, rise and tack-free from mix start.

5. Do not touch after cream. Cut at 24 h.

First cup: 50.00 g B (includes 5.00 g HFO) + 41.40 g A on the 50 g lab sheet. No machine spray until three clean cups.

Working equations

EWpolyol = 56 100 / OH#  ·  eq = pbw / EW  ·  EWwater = 9.0  ·  EWPMDI = 4 200 / %NCO = 135.5 at 31.0 %

Pay water NCO first, then polyol OH. Never pour 1:1 on a high-water open-cell cup.

Index = (NCO eq / OH eq) × 100  ·  A-side (g) = OH eq × (Index / 100) × EWPMDI

Record

Temperature, mix time, cream, gel, rise, tack-free, collapse, shrink, scorch, friability.

Measure

Free-rise density. Then compressive strength, 70 °C / 90 % RH stability, closed-cell content if you can.

Pass

Three cups, no collapse, density in window, cream/rise pair repeats.

These are laboratory starting formulas, not a production specification or a fire certificate. Confirm silicone grade (open-cell vs closed-cell), FR grade, and the exact PMDI %NCO before you lock a plant card.
