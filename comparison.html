What to do in the lab

Keep from this pack

Pour BX-01 first. Use CC-01 only if BX-01 is too tight. Use BX-02 if sucrose is on the floor. Use OC-01 only as the high-water memo screen. Do not pour Rev. F CC-F1.

Take from cup-test v4

If Mannich/sucrose, polyethers, HFO-1233zd, K-15 / DMCHA and a KIMSPRAY-style target are available, v4 OC (water 4.5, density 18–24) and v4 CC (HFO 10, water 1.1, density 35–40) are closer to commercial spray. Run both bases as 100 g B-side cups and keep one variable at a time.

Closed-cell HFO-1233zd(E) is mandatory. This pack uses one TB-307 or PE 104 drum and one TEROL 250 or PE 103 drum. Cup-test v4 still writes the Flokser names and a different catalyst set. Do not blend the two OC-01 formulas into one pour.
