What to do in the lab

Keep from this pack

Use CC-01 if the only polyols on the floor are CREAPOL 326 / 1500 / PD 230 and the PO catalysts PC-15 / PC-37 / CAT-T. Use OC-01 only as a high-water memo screen, or OC-02 if sucrose/polyether has not arrived.

Take from cup-test v4

If Mannich/sucrose, polyethers, HFO-1233zd, K-15 / DMCHA and a KIMSPRAY-style target are available, v4 OC (water 4.5, density 18–24) and v4 CC (HFO 10, water 1.1, density 35–40) are closer to commercial spray. Run both bases as 100 g B-side cups and keep one variable at a time.

Same CREAPOL grades appear in both. The big splits are open-cell water (24 vs 4.5), whether CC starts with HFO, and which catalyst family is used. Do not blend the two OC-01 formulas into one pour.
